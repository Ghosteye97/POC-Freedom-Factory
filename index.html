02A / ABOUT THE TRACK

FROM DESOTO
TO FREEDOM.

Freedom Factory is a revived 3/8-mile oval in Bradenton, Florida, with 12-degree banking and roots that reach back to the old Desoto Speedway days. The track carries real short-track history, now reworked into a modern home for racing, drifting, burnouts, and big-event weekends.

That mix is what makes the place special: old-school Florida racing heritage up front, full-throttle Cleetus energy in the stands, and a venue that can swing from circle-track competition to smoke-filled spectacle without losing its identity.
