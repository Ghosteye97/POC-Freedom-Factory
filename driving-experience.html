BUYER QUESTIONS

TURN THE CURRENT FAQ INTO A CLEAR SALES TOOL
Who can drive?

Drivers must be 16 or older with a valid license. Drivers under 18 need a guardian waiver.

Need experience?

No prior racing background is required. The program is built so first-timers can arrive excited and leave confident.

Can family watch?

Yes. Friends and family can be part of the day, making the experience feel bigger than just the laps themselves.

Passengers?

No passengers are allowed due to insurance. That needs to be easy to spot before purchase.

Weather policy?

If weather interrupts the event, guests can expect rescheduling or refund guidance from the track team.

Merch + food?

Merch is available on property, and guests should plan ahead in case concessions are limited on certain dates.
